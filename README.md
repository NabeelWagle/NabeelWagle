## About

I work at the point where business needs meet engineering reality. My background covers business analysis, software engineering, and quality execution, which means I write requirements that developers can build from and I test whether what was built actually does what the business asked for.

Most of my career has been on enterprise systems: a case management platform used by UK police forces, a patient management system for hospitals, and a property management app. Those projects taught me to work with stakeholders, engineering teams, and deadlines at the same time.

I am now deepening my AI and machine learning work through an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at HKUST (2026 to 2027). I care about full stack development, clean product engineering, and building AI features that people can trust and use.

**Open To**
- Business Analyst and PMO roles in Hong Kong, including AI and data-focused positions
- Contract or temporary roles in analysis, delivery, and product work
- Collaboration on applied AI and data projects
